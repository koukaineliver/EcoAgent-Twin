# Dependencies and data

The source archive is available under its source-code license. It uses external
dependencies including SUMO, Mesa, Ray RLlib, PyTorch, OR-Tools, and LangGraph.
Install those dependencies under their respective terms. The optional
SmolLM2-135M-Instruct checkpoint is downloaded separately from its pinned model
card at https://huggingface.co/HuggingFaceTB/SmolLM2-135M-Instruct.

The included aggregate and case-level comparison files were generated on
synthetic cases. The Amazon Last-Mile Routing Research Challenge data has its
own noncommercial terms and is obtained separately at
https://registry.opendata.aws/amazon-last-mile-challenges/.

OpenStreetMap road database terms are documented in runs/osmnx_la_university_park/LICENSE-OSM.txt.
